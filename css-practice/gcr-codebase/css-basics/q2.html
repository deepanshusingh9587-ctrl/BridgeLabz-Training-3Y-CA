About Jaipur

Jaipur is the capital city of Rajasthan. It is also known as the Pink City because of its beautiful pink-colored buildings.

Famous Places

Jaipur is famous for places such as Hawa Mahal, Amber Fort, City Palace, and Jantar Mantar.

Culture

Jaipur has a rich culture, traditional food, colorful festivals, music, and handicrafts.
